## The app

Unscroll is free to use for one tracked app, with every feature included: the live timer, limits, the swipe limit, blocking, stats and the fox. Your history and data are never locked, whatever plan you are on.

## Unscroll Plus

- **What it adds:** tracking for every supported app instead of one.
- **Price:** shown in the app and in Google Play before you buy, in your currency. It is billed monthly by Google Play.
- **Renewal:** the subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel.
- **Cancelling:** cancel any time in Google Play (Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions, or Settings › Unscroll Plus › Manage subscription in the app). You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Refunds:** handled by Google Play under its refund policies.
- **When Plus ends:** nothing is deleted. The app you picked (or your most used one) stays tracked; the others are paused, with their history and settings kept. Subscribe again and they resume as they were.
- **Payments:** Google Play handles all payments. Unscroll has no accounts and no servers, and never sees your payment details or sends your usage data anywhere.

## No warranty

Unscroll is provided "as is". It helps you notice and limit your time in social apps, but it can't guarantee that a limit or block is never bypassed (for example after a force stop or a revoked permission).
