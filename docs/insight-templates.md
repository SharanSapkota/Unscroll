# Unscroll Insights: template bank

130+ message templates. The app fills in the {placeholders} from real data, so every message is true and no two look the same.

Tone tags: **[G]** gentle, **[H]** honest, **[B]** brutal. Users pick their tone in Settings (default: Honest). Brutal is opt-in. Never show [B] messages on a user's first 3 days or right after a good day.

## Rotation rules

- Never repeat a template within its last 30 uses.
- Never show the same category twice in a row.
- Match the user's tone setting. [G] always allowed, [H] for Honest and Brutal, [B] only for Brutal.
- Don't show negative messages on days under the limit. Use Wins instead.
- Life projection (section 3): at most once per week.
- If a placeholder has no data, skip that template and try another.
- No health, body, or diet claims anywhere.
